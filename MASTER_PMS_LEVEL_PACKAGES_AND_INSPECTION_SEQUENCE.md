## 🏗️ Physical Garage Bay Sequence Mapping

To minimize mechanic movement and maximize garage bay throughput, inspections follow a strict physical 3-stage flow:

```
[ Stage 1: Under Hood & ECU ] ──> [ Stage 2: Wheels & Brakes ] ──> [ Stage 3: Under Hoist & Chassis ]
```

1. **Stage 1 — Under Hood & Cabin (Hood Open, Vehicle Ground Level):**
   - Connect OBD-II scanner for ECU Diagnostic Scan.
   - Inspect battery, ignition spark plugs, air filter, cabin filter, drivebelts, and fluid levels.
2. **Stage 2 — Wheels, Tires & Brakes (Mid-Height Lift):**
   - Remove wheels. Inspect brake pad thickness, rotors, caliper slide pins, drum linings, and wheel cylinders.
3. **Stage 3 — Under Hoist & Under Chassis (Full Height Lift):**
   - Drain engine oil/transmission fluid; replace oil filter.
   - Inspect underchassis suspension bushings, ball joints, tie rods, rack and pinion, shocks, and engine/transmission mounts.
